CONFIRMED ROOT CAUSE (no further investigation needed): GymDetailModal.tsx's 머신
tab renders ALL `filteredMachines` synchronously via `<ScrollView><View style={s.machineGrid}>{filteredMachines.map(...)}</View></ScrollView>`,
with no virtualization and no per-item memoization. Every filter chip press or any
parent re-render re-renders every single MachineCard (which does SVG border drawing
+ image loading), and the list never virtualizes off-screen items. FindGymScreen.tsx
avoids this entirely with a virtualized FlatList + React.memo item component. This
task brings GymDetailModal.tsx in line with that proven pattern. Do NOT modify
MachineCard.tsx in this task — it stays exactly as-is, only GymDetailModal.tsx's
container/rendering strategy changes.
